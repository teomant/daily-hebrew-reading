# Project instructions

These project rules reflect the owner's explicit direction on October 28, 2026.
They replace the earlier global review requirements for this project.

## Work and review

- Finish the authorized work without repeated approval or review cycles.
- Use one focused direct review before handoff.
  Do not run independent or repeated reviews unless the owner explicitly requests them for the current task.
- Plan broad changes before implementation, but keep planning proportionate.
  Remove temporary task plan documents before handoff unless the owner asks to keep them.
- Update the implementation log and affected product documentation.
- Run relevant offline checks.
  Do not run paid generation or deploy merely to verify a local change.

## Generation quality

- Publishing usable imperfect articles is better than publishing nothing.
- Improve reading levels, dialogue consistency, topic variety, and lexical segmentation through clear prompts, local checks, and bounded targeted repairs.
- Keep successful text and levels when another part needs repair.
- Treat remaining dialogue-style and segmentation defects as logged warnings when the article meets the public schema, translation-coverage, identity, and required factual-coverage contracts.
  Do not discard a usable article solely because a quality repair fails.
- Do not weaken factual accuracy, invent missing facts, or hide failed checks.
- Keep API usage and estimated cost visible in logs.
