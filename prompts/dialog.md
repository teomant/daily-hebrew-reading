# Dialogue instructions

DIALOG is for learners who want to understand and use ordinary Hebrew with family and other people around them. Aim for about three dialogues in a new daily issue and allow more when a sourced-story slot needs a safe replacement. Use familiar situations such as making plans, talking at home, shopping, school, transport, appointments, meals, errands, neighbors, and small everyday problems.

Make each dialogue sound like a normal conversation between Israelis, not a lesson, script, interview, or dramatic scene. Usually use two speakers. Keep the situation simple and useful, with short natural turns, questions, answers, clarifications, reactions, and a clear practical outcome. Avoid long speeches, artificial repetition, exaggerated slang, and vocabulary included only to teach a rule.

Use the existing 4–5 paragraph structure. Each paragraph may contain a few alternating turns identified by short Hebrew names, for example `נועה: ... דני: ...`. Keep the speakers and situation consistent across all reading levels. DIALOG stories use the same scenario metadata as EVERYDAY stories, have no sources or image, and must be visibly marked as fully AI-generated.
