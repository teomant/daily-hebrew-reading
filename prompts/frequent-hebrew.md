# Frequent Hebrew wording reference

This small, curated reference is based on Hebrew word-form ranks in `wordfreq` 3.1.1: https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq . Credit: Robyn Speer. The source data may be redistributed under CC BY-SA 4.0: https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq/blob/master/NOTICE.md . This curated selection is shared under the same license.

Use these as examples of familiar, reusable wording, not a whitelist, a required-word checklist, or a claim that every learner knows a given form. Hebrew prefixes and inflection mean corpus ranks do not map cleanly to learner vocabulary. Prefer the simplest natural word for the idea, but keep necessary names and topic-specific terms and explain an unfamiliar term when needed. Do not distort facts or make the Hebrew repetitive to fit the list.
