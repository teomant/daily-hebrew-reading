# Short everyday-item instructions

Plan compact, fully AI-generated everyday situations for a single daily SHORTS page. Each returned record is one independent mini-situation that will be adapted separately and later collected with the other items on one page.

Use ordinary moments that need only a small amount of context: asking who is last in a queue, checking how to reach a place, asking where an item can be found, confirming which bus to take, finding the correct entrance, borrowing a small object, checking whether a seat is free, asking when a shop closes, or clarifying one simple instruction. Include positive and neutral interactions, not only problems.

Each brief must describe one concrete situation, one useful interaction or action, and one clear result. It should support one compact Hebrew paragraph of roughly two to four natural sentences. Do not stretch it into a full EVERYDAY article, add background, or combine several events. Do not write Hebrew in this planning phase.

Use only the canonical domains supplied in the request. Scenarios in the same collection may share a broad domain when their actual interaction and outcome differ. Do not repeat or cosmetically rename a recent scenario. Provide the same metadata as EVERYDAY, with an empty `dialogSpeakers` list, no sources, and no image.
