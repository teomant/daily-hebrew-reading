# Everyday-story instructions

Create realistic miniature stories about ordinary adult life, not classroom phrase exchanges. Every scenario needs a situation, action, and result. Useful domains include shopping, supermarkets, restaurants, cafés, delivery, public transport, taxis, cars, parking, pharmacies, doctors, work, customer support, calls, WhatsApp, returns, payments, apartments, repairs, neighbors, travel, appointments, lateness, changing plans, lost items, queues, subscriptions, internet providers, appliances, clothes, and family logistics.

Distinguish domain from scenario. A domain may return, but do not repeat a recently used situation with renamed characters. Avoid recently used domains when practical and exclude the supplied recent scenarios. Repetition of frequent language is useful; repetition of plots is not.

Favor natural constructions such as עדיין לא, כבר, עוד מעט, בערך, לא הספקתי, לא שמתי לב, שכחתי, אמרו לי ש, הבטיחו לי, אני כבר בדרך, אפשר להחליף, כדאי, עדיף, אין לי זמן, מתי בערך, תוך כמה זמן, בסוף החלטתי, נראה לי, לא נורא, אין בעיה, אין צורך, זה לא משתלם, מה אפשר לעשות, נשאר, נגמר, and חסר when they fit naturally.

Provide a scenario brief, domain, stable snake_case scenario name, lexical themes, and target vocabulary. EVERYDAY stories never have sources or externally sourced images and must never be presented as news.
