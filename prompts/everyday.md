# Everyday-story instructions

Plan fully AI-generated EVERYDAY scenarios. This phase creates only English briefs and scenario metadata; do not research factual stories, use web sources, write Hebrew adaptations, segment text, or translate anything. Create realistic miniature stories about ordinary adult life, not classroom phrase exchanges. Every scenario needs a situation, a natural interaction between people, an action, and a result. Prioritize language learners can reuse with colleagues, managers, customers, neighbors, friends, strangers, and service workers. Useful domains include office conversations, meetings, schedule changes, asking for help or directions, misunderstandings, small talk, invitations, shopping, supermarkets, restaurants, cafés, delivery, public transport, taxis, parking, pharmacies, doctors, customer support, calls, WhatsApp, returns, payments, apartments, repairs, neighbors, appointments, lateness, lost items, queues, clothes, and family logistics.

Distinguish domain from scenario. A domain may return, but do not repeat a recently used situation with renamed characters. Treat every supplied recent scenario record as forbidden comparison data. An identical `scenario` value is automatically a duplicate and must never be returned. A renamed scenario is still a duplicate when the practical problem or goal, interaction, and resolution are substantially the same. Avoid recently used domains when practical and exclude the supplied recent scenarios. Repetition of frequent language is useful; repetition of plots is not.

Favor natural constructions such as עדיין לא, כבר, עוד מעט, בערך, לא הספקתי, לא שמתי לב, שכחתי, אמרו לי ש, הבטיחו לי, אני כבר בדרך, אפשר להחליף, כדאי, עדיף, אין לי זמן, מתי בערך, תוך כמה זמן, בסוף החלטתי, נראה לי, לא נורא, אין בעיה, אין צורך, זה לא משתלם, מה אפשר לעשות, נשאר, נגמר, and חסר when they fit naturally. Include realistic requests, clarifications, polite disagreement, explanations, reactions, and changes of plan—not only narration.

Make each scenario brief adaptation-ready by laying out 4–5 distinct story beats: the initial situation, the interaction or first attempt, a useful clarification or complication, the decision or action, and the practical outcome. Give the later adaptation enough concrete but ordinary material for developed paragraphs without filler. Provide the brief, domain, stable snake_case scenario name, lexical themes, and target vocabulary. EVERYDAY stories never have sources or images and must never be presented as news.
